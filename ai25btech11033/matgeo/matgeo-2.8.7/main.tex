\textbf{Question}:\\

\noindent Find $\overrightarrow|{\vec{a}}$ . $\overrightarrow{\vec{b}|}$ , if two vectors 
$\overrightarrow{\vec{a}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\vec{b}}$ are such that $\overrightarrow|{\vec{a}|}$ =2, $\overrightarrow|{\vec{b}|}$ =3 and $\overrightarrow{\vec{a}}$. 
$\overrightarrow{\vec{b}}$=4 

\textbf{solution}:\\


Angle between vectors ;

\begin{align}
 |\vec{a}| &= 2 \\
|\vec{b}| &= 3 \\
\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} &= 4
\end{align}

Matrix interpretation of dot product;

\begin{align}
    \overrightarrow{\vec{a}}. \overrightarrow{\vec{b}} = \overrightarrow{{a^T}} . \overrightarrow{{b}} 
\end{align}
And the cosine of the angle $\theta$ btween $\overrightarrow{\vec{a}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\vec{b}}$ is:

\begin{align}
   \cos \theta=\frac{a^T b}{\|\vec{a}\|\|\vec{b}\|}
\end{align}

Compute $\cos \theta$

\begin{align}
       \cos \theta=\frac{4}{2.3}=\frac{2}{3}
\end{align}

Angle ;

\begin{align}
\theta=\frac{2}{3}
\end{align}
